Hollywood Animal Database

Hollywood Animal is an Early Access movie studio management game developed by Weappy Studio.

In this game you play as the new owner of a bankrupt movie studio in 1929, with the goal of turning this old shipwreck of the past into something worth your money. Will you spend big time to make the biggest movies that everyone will see? Will you reject conformity and make films worth getting killed over? Or will you deal in immorality while paying the cop to look the other way? The choice is yours in this simulacrum of the light and dark sides of the hollywoodian world. A place of dreams,
